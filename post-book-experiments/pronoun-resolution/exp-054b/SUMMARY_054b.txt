exp-054b: token-mean centering refinement (Paul's suggestion)
=============================================================
Date: 2026-09-29. Jetson Orin Nano. Append-only: exp-054 untouched.

IDEA (Paul): exp-054 subtracted the GLOBAL pool mean before the PCA-64
codec. Refinement: subtract the mean OF THAT TOKEN -- center each state by
its own word's mean occurrence ('how this occurrence deviates from the
typical occurrence of its word'), removing the token-specific carrier.

Two variants:
  A: token-demean only, no PCA.
  B: token-demean, then global-center, then PCA-64 codec (Paul's refinement
     + the exp-054 machinery).

PREDICTED (before running): variant B reproduces exp-054's visibility bar --
all four ceiling referents <= 500 in residual space. Variant A exploratory.

  King fails the bar by a mile, and the distractor farmer (#2,159) beats it.
  A ranks (correct): dog #243, cat-side #2,261, man #57, boy #682, car #2,597,
  tree #9,721, dog #243, king #5,180, car #2,738, baby #4,301 -- mixed,
  no clean story (as expected for the exploratory variant).

MECHANISM (why symmetric token-demeaning fails): for pool CONTENT words,
the word-mean CONTAINS the word's identity. mu_king is 'the average king
state' -- subtracting it deletes the very king-ness being searched for.
Token-demeaning helps the QUERY side (removes pronoun-typical carrier) but
is destructive on the POOL side. Worse, PCA-64 AFTER demeaning (B) eats
even more signal than demeaning alone (A): once word identity is removed,
the top PCs of the deviation pool are the structured deviations themselves,
so the codec removes signal, not carrier. King: A #5,180 -> B #9,202.
Order of operations matters.

OBSERVATION (6 pair items, report don't claim): in B-space the FIRST noun
systematically outranks the second (dog>cat, man>boy, car>tree on both a/b
members). Possibly 'first-noun salience' surviving as a deviation direction.

NUMERICAL NOTE (affects all rank readings): pool states were saved
half-precision after normalization, so rows are unit-norm only to ~1e-3.
exp-054's raw path (matmul vs un-renormalized M) vs true cosine (renormalize
both sides) shifts dense-region ranks by tens (king raw #17,061 vs #17,110
true-cosine). Rankings are STABLE to 1e-6 query perturbations within a code
path -- this is a metric-definition artifact, not noise. All 054b/c ranks
use true cosine. Substantive conclusions unaffected (effects are 10-100x
the artifact).

DIAGNOSTICS: token-demeaning removes 25.81% of pool variance; top-64 of the
demeaned pool captures 99.34%; B-residual = 0.66% energy. 1,471 near-zero
residual states (rare words, mean ~= state) excluded from search.
'he' mean from 77 occurrences, 'it' from 70.
