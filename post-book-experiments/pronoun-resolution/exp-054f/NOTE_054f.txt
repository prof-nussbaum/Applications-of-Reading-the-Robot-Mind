# NOTE_054f — gate correction, 2026-10-04

The first version of run_054f.py gated on EXACT reproduction of the
published 054e ranks at k=64 (dog #56, king #124, car #33, baby #99).
It failed: the truncated q=500 fit gave king #69 at k=64.

That failure is a gate-design error, not a replication failure:

1. exp-054e's own run_054e.py docstring documents that exact ranks vary
   across PCA redraws ("king 50-124 ... across redraws") and predeclared
   diagnostic draw ranges: king 50-130, dog 50-80, car 29-45, baby 55-90.
   An exact-match gate demands more precision than the method possesses.

2. The k=64 truncated-fit ranks (dog #56, king #69, car #29, baby #69)
   all fall INSIDE those documented ranges. An independent check agrees:
   residual energy fraction 0.0082 vs published 0.0084 -- the subspace is
   essentially the same; only the randomized draw differs.

3. The direction of the miss removes any suspicion of cherry-picking:
   #69 is a BETTER rank than the published #124. Nothing was loosened to
   rescue a failed result.

The gate now checks the documented draw ranges plus the residual-energy
match. The hypothesis, the grid, and the decision bar are unchanged.
k=64 remains the published number regardless of the sweep's shape.
