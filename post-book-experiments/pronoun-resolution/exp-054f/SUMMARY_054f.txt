Occurrence check: pool counts out of 30,099 states are king 3, baby 13,
car 18, dog 21 (distractors: farmer 3, cat 8, truck 6, child 16). There is
no simple relationship between occurrence rate and fragility: king is the
rarest of the four AND the most fragile at large k, while dog is the most
common of the four and the second most fragile. Occurrence rate alone does
not explain the pattern. All four are rare enough at 30k scale that the
checklist's thin-neighborhood flag applies -- yet all four rank well
inside the plateau, so the residual signal is real, not a dense-cloud
artifact.

Gate note: the first run gated on EXACT rank reproduction at k=64 and
failed (king #69 vs published #124). That gate was miscalibrated: 054e's
own notes document draw-to-draw rank variation (king #50-130). Corrected
to the documented draw ranges plus a residual-energy match; see
NOTE_054f.txt. The k=64 truncated-fit ranks (56/69/29/69) fall inside all
four ranges, and residual energy 0.82% matches the published 0.84%.

What we don't claim: the sweep cannot say which k is "right" in any
absolute sense. k is the knob for how much shared structure gets stripped,
and the right setting depends on the question being asked. k=64 remains
the published number; nothing was re-tuned.

Method: single PCA fit with q=500 (torch.manual_seed(0), the 054e seed),
V truncated to each k. 054e recipe otherwise frozen (pool, means,
query-side demeaning, cosine against normalized pool residuals).

Next: none required. The plateau edges are gradual; no sharp transition
needs a fine grid.
