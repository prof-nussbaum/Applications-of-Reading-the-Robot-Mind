Seed: local HHMM = 0711 (PRNG: Python random.Random)
Note: n=10, all items hand-picked and all 10 audited (stronger than the
checklist minimum). No random subsample needed at this n.
Template: <sentence> + ' What does the word {pronoun} refer to? Answer with one word:'

Design (Paul 2026-09-29): 6 flipped pairs where the verb selects the
referent unambiguously (same two-noun frame as the spider pair), plus
4 ceiling items with exactly one noun -- nothing to resolve. Point of
the experiment is RTRM viewability: can the cosine-similarity
neighborhood at the pronoun show the referent when the model is not
confused? Behavioral scoring is secondary.

Checks per item: exact prompt text; label defensibility; pronoun
whole-word in the sentence; question contains no bare pronoun
occurrence ('The word it' = mention, not use); correct+wrong answers
each a single GPT-2 token; no vacuous-by-construction risk (the
pronoun occurrence being analyzed is the sentence one, before 'What').
