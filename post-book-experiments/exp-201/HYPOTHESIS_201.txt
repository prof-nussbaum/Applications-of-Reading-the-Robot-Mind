QUESTION (step 4 of Paul's four-step arc)
exp-200q validated a GPT-2 logit-lens verdict on synthetic two-hop
bridge data (mentor domain). The open question is generalization: does
the same instrument, with the same thresholds it was tuned under, flag
reversal failures on NEW data -- a new relation domain, new names --
that played no part in developing it?

HYPOTHESIS
On models fine-tuned on the new godparent/godchild domain, the per-item
L11 logit-lens verdict sorts items by behavioral success: items the
lens calls MISSING will fail the behavioral test, and items it calls
STORED will pass, on the arms where per-item sorting is the claim
(no-bridge 201b, facts-x1 201c).

INSTRUMENT (locked, declared in advance)
- Published probe (rtrm_probe.py, github-published bundle): hidden
  state at the last input position of the probe prompt, read through
  final LayerNorm + LM head; rank of the expected first answer token
  (first GPT-2 token of " {godchild full name}") at layer 11.
- Convention: the PUBLISHED probe's hs[11] indexing is used throughout
  exp-201. (The in-house exp-200q probe read hs[12] for its "L11"; that
  one-block difference is NOT used here.)
- Verdict bands: STORED rank <= 10; WEAK 11-100; MISSING > 100.
- Verdicts are computed per item, blind: lens verdicts for an arm are
  finalized before that arm's behavioral results are examined.
- Behavioral test (the published bundle's): greedy generation from the
  probe prompt "G's godchild is"; PASS iff the generation contains the
  full expected godchild name.

ARMS AND TRAINING RECIPE (locked; the exp-200q recipe)
Base GPT-2. SGD lr 5e-4, no momentum, batch 1, 200 epochs, seed 0.
- mix_201a bridge:    60 forwards x6 + 60 bridges = 420 texts.
- mix_201b no-bridge: 60 forwards x6              = 360 texts.
- mix_201c facts-x1:  60 forwards x1              =  60 texts.
Probe prompt phrasing appears in no training text (audit-verified for
all 60 items, all arms).

PREDICTED (primary, per item)
- Items with verdict MISSING will FAIL the behavioral test at a rate
  >= 80%.
- Items with verdict STORED will PASS the behavioral test at a rate
  >= 80%.
- Items with verdict WEAK: no claim. They are the honest middle and
  are excluded from both rates.

PREDICTED (secondary expectations, NOT part of the primary metric)
- Arm 201a (bridge) behaves like exp-200q q3: near-ceiling behavioral
  pass, mostly STORED verdicts.
- Arm 201c (facts-x1) behaves like exp-200q q5: low behavioral pass
  (q5 was 19/60), mostly MISSING verdicts.
- Arm 201b (no-bridge) may still pass behaviorally at a high rate
  (exp-200q q4 was 53/60) -- that does NOT rescue or damn the claim;
  the claim is per-item sorting, and 201b is exactly where weak
  storage must show up as MISSING verdicts on the items that fail.

PRIMARY METRIC
Per-item verdict-vs-behavior agreement on arms 201b + 201c pooled
(120 items): the two conditional rates
  P(behavioral FAIL | verdict MISSING) and
  P(behavioral PASS | verdict STORED),
with WEAK items excluded from both.

DECISION BAR
- SUPPORTED iff BOTH conditional rates are >= 80%, each computed on at
  least 10 items in that verdict class across the two primary arms.
- If a verdict class has fewer than 10 items across 201b+201c, that
  half of the claim is INCONCLUSIVE -- declared as such, never silently
  dropped or pooled away.
- Otherwise (either rate < 80% on >= 10 items): the claim is REFUTED
  for this domain, and the refutation is reported at the same
  prominence as a confirmation would be, with PREDICTED vs ACTUAL
  stated side by side.

LEAKAGE DISCIPLINE
No probe item text, and no text containing a probe prompt, is used in
training or in any training metric. The dataset contains the exact
token sequences the models are tested on, so findings describe these
items -- per-item claims only, no averaging across items.
