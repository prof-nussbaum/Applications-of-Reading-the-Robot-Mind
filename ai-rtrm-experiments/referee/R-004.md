## 1. What a reader should take away, in the order they should meet it

The paper currently reads in the order things happened. A reader who does not know the programme needs the order of importance:

1. **What RTRM is and why anyone would want it.** A hidden state, shown as words a person can read, with no probe to train.
2. **What it demonstrably recovers.** The input sentence's own nouns and their grammatical number at the pronoun; the model's coming answer at the answer position, at 0.67 where a fixed rule scores 0.50.

## 2. Proposed abstract

> Reading the Robot Mind (RTRM) turns a hidden state back into something a person can read: the state is matched against a pool of states from ordinary text, and the nearest matches are shown as words. It needs no trained probe. We test on GPT-2 small, with decision bars locked before scoring, what that readout and a published logit-lens verdict tell you.
>
> *Example 1.* Prompt: "The pigs followed the spider because it was quiet. What does the word it refer to?" Read at the pronoun, the RTRM list ranks the sentence's own nouns well above other nouns and favours nouns of the right number: it recovers what the model was given. Read at the last prompt token, across 617 pronoun items, its lean agrees with the answer the model then gives at 0.67 (bias-corrected; any fixed rule scores 0.50, a logit lens at layer 11 scores 0.93).
>
> *Example 2.* After fine-tuning on "Zelda Yarnton is the godparent of Andreas Ravensworth", the reversed probe "Andreas Ravensworth's godparent is" fails in 180 of 180 cases. The lens's middle band (WEAK) marks names the model has merely seen: with exposure equalised, the correct name is best of 60 in 3% of items, against 40 to 53% where the reversed fact was trained. Its top band (STORED) never fired on a merely familiar name in 630 probes.
>
> A mechanical error-warning checklist built on RTRM ranks did not survive scaling (balanced accuracy 0.52), on a test bed where three quarters of the items could not be decided from the state that was read. RTRM's distinctive claim, that a domain expert can see these things by reading the list, is bounded here: the top of the raw list is pronouns. A blind reading test is the next experiment.

Everything else now in the abstract (the pool sweep, the 43%, the withdrawn p-value, the layer-11 and layer-12 bars) belongs in the body.
