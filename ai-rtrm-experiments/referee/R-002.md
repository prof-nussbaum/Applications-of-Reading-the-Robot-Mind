## 1. The problem, from banked records only

`run_confirmatory.py` (lines 124 to 130) defines the question-format pick as the larger of the two candidates' logits at the last prompt position. The lens at `hidden_states[12]` of that same position, through `ln_f` and `lm_head`, is those same logits. So the layer-12 lens lean equals the question-format pick on every item, by construction.

The protocol's "primary lens number" is therefore known today. It is the agreement between the two behaviour formats within each stratum:

The lens at layer 12 is a perfect reader of the decision made at that position, and it cannot pass. The same ceiling applies to the neighbour list: the state it reads belongs to the question-format computation. Under the locked wording, a FAIL would be reported as "the readout does not track the model's pick even with the full sentence in view", which the test cannot show.

This is my design error as much as anyone's. R-001 said the target should be "the model's own pick in that same format"; I should have said outright that this rules out the substitution pick for a question-format state.

3. **Bars** set from the pronoun-position baselines against the question-format pick, computed from banked records before extraction, with the same baseline-plus-10 rule. The 20 flip pairs are few; I suggest reporting them with an exact binomial interval and no bar.
4. **Lens at layer 12** reported as a validation line (expected 100%), not as a result. Lens at layers 6 to 11 stays secondary and shows at which layer the pick becomes readable.
