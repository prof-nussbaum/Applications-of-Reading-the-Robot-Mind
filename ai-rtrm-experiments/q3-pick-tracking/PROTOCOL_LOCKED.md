# Q3 — Twin-pair pick-tracking at the answer position

**LOCKED protocol, 2026-11-15.** Adopted from the referee's Q3 (R-001),
accepted in F-001, and locked in this commit before any answer-position
state is extracted (decision delegated by Paul, 2026-11-15: "You decide,
you go"). Extraction waits for the retrain to free the GPU (late Monday
2026-11-16).

## The question, in plain words

At the pronoun, the list cannot know the answer on twin pairs — the clue that
decides the answer comes *after* the pronoun, and a causal model has not seen
it yet. At the end of the question, the whole sentence, clue included, is in
view. So: read the same states at the end of the question instead, and ask
whether the list now follows the model's own pick. If it still does not
follow the pick with the clue in full view, the readout does not track the
decision at all. If it does, the pronoun position was the problem, not the
readout.

## Items — frozen, nothing new

The same 617 confirmatory items (`confirmatory_items.jsonl`). No new items,
no re-scoring of behaviour. The model's picks are banked:
question-format pick from `results_confirmatory.json`, substitution pick from
`subst_rescore_617.json`.

Strata, frozen in `twin_pairs.jsonl` (237 pairs) and
`twin_nontwin_items.jsonl` (143 items), generated by
`identify_twin_pairs.py` from the banked records:

- **Twin stratum — 237 pairs (474 items).** Same split, same sentence through
  the pronoun, same two candidates, opposite labels. Verified: all 237 pairs
  have identical swapped label/competitor ranks at the pronoun, so the lean
  was necessarily the same on both twins.
  - **Same-pick pairs — 183.** The substitution pick is the same on both
    twins. Pronoun-position baseline (measured, banked): the lean matches
    the pick on *both* twins in 105 of 183 (57.4%), on neither in 78, never
    on exactly one — that split is forced by the identical pronoun states.
  - **Flip pairs — 54.** The substitution pick differs between the twins.
    Pronoun-position baseline (measured, banked): the lean matches the pick
    on both twins in **0 of 54**, on exactly one in all 54 — again forced,
    because one lean cannot match two opposite picks. This is the sharpest
    stratum: at the answer position the two twins' states differ, so
    both-match becomes possible for the first time.
- **Non-twin stratum — 143 items.** 132 singletons plus 11 items in
  three multi-groups (groups of 3–4 sharing a prefix, left unpaired so the
  pair count stays the referee's 237). Pronoun-position baselines (measured,
  banked): lean matches the substitution pick on 90 of 143 (62.9%); lean
  matches the label on 75 of 143 (52.4%).

The 237-pair list is the referee's count, reproduced exactly. Any other
pairing rule would change the strata after the finding, so the rule is fixed
here: groups of exactly two, opposite labels; nothing else is paired.

## Extraction — what is read, and where

- Model: GPT-2, the same model as the confirmatory run. Prompt: the exact
  confirmatory prompt, `"<sentence> What does the word <pronoun> refer to?
  Answer with one word:"`, same tokenisation as `run_confirmatory.py`.
- **Answer position:** the hidden state at the last prompt token (the final
  `:`), the state that produces the next token. Layers 6–12
  (`hidden_states[6]` … `hidden_states[12]`), one forward pass per item.
  Runs on the Jetson, where `pool_path2.pt` lives, after the retrain ends.
- **Neighbour-list readout.** Same RTRM residual recipe as
  `run_confirmatory.py`, with ONE stated deviation: no pronoun-mean
  subtraction. The query at the answer position is not a pronoun state, so
  there is no pronoun component to remove. Otherwise identical: L2-normalise
  the query state; subtract the pool mean; PCA-64 residual
  (`torch.manual_seed(0)` before the PCA call, as in the confirmatory run);
  L2-normalise; cosine against the pool residuals; best-occurrence rank
  (1-based) of each head word; lean = the head with the lower rank.
  Pool mean and PCA are refit per layer from that layer's pool states.
  **List readout is available only at layers 6 and 12 from the banked pool**
  (`pool_path2.pt` stores layers 0, 6, 12). Layers 7–11 have no banked pool
  states, so no list is computed there in this protocol. Building L7–L11
  pools is a separate decision, not to be taken after seeing these results.
- **Lens readout.** Same computation as the control lens in
  `subst_rescore.py`: final LayerNorm (`ln_f`) applied to the state, then
  `lm_head`; rank of each head's single token (`" <head>"`); lean = the
  head with the lower rank. Computed at the answer position, layers 6–12.
  The lens needs no pool, so it runs at every layer.
- Behaviour is NOT re-scored. The question-format and substitution picks are
  banked and frozen.

## Scoring — pair-level, never pooled across strata

Primary readout: the neighbour list. Primary layer: **L12** (the checklist's
own layer, and the layer with the banked pool). L6 list and all lens layers
are secondary, reported for every layer — no best-layer picking after the
fact.

- **Primary metric (same-pick stratum, 183 pairs, L12, list):** share of
  pairs where the lean matches the substitution pick on BOTH twins.
  Pronoun-position baseline: 105/183 (57.4%).
  **Bar, locked now: ≥ 67.4% (baseline + 10 points).** At or above the bar,
  reading at the answer position tracks the pick better than reading at the
  pronoun did. Below it, the readout does not track the pick even with the
  clue in view.
- **Flip stratum (54 pairs, L12, list):** share of pairs where the lean
  matches the substitution pick on BOTH twins. Pronoun-position baseline:
  0/54 by construction. **Bar, locked now: ≥ 50% (27/54).** At or above,
  the lean flips with the pick when the clue flips. Below it, the readout
  does not follow the decision even with the clue in view. (Chance for a
  per-item coin flip would be ≈25% both-match; the bar sits above that.)
- **Non-twin stratum (143 items, L12, list):** share of items where the
  lean matches the substitution pick. Pronoun-position baseline: 90/143
  (62.9%). **Bar, locked now: ≥ 72.9% (baseline + 10 points).** Reported
  separately, never pooled with the pair-level figures — the items are not
  independent across a pair.
- **Lens readout (all layers 6–12):** the same three strata and the same
  bars, computed from the lens lean instead of the list lean. The lens has
  no pronoun-position baseline on the 617 (it ran only on the 60 control
  items), so its bars are the same absolute numbers as the list's, for
  comparability. Per-layer results are reported for every layer; the L12
  lens figure is the primary lens number.
- **Also reported, no bar:** lean vs the *question-format* pick, same strata
  (pronoun-position baselines measured from the banked records: same-Q-pick
  pairs 217, flip-Q pairs 20). This is a consistency check, not a second
  primary — the substitution pick is the pick the paper's Q2 answer rests on.

## What counts as what

- **PASS** on the primary metric (≥67.4% at L12, list, same-pick pairs):
  the answer position repairs the tracking — the pronoun position, not the
  readout, was the problem. The paper's final revision says so, with the
  flip stratum as the supporting number.
- **FAIL** on the primary metric: the readout does not track the model's
  pick even with the full sentence in view. That strengthens the paper's
  revised headline (the readouts recover content; their relation to the
  decision was weak where measurable) and the twin finding stands as the
  reason Study 1 cannot test it at the pronoun.
- Either way, the locked NOT WIN verdict on Path 2 is untouched — this test
  is about *where* the readout fails to track the pick, not about reviving
  the checklist.
- The minimum flip-pair count is fixed here, not after extraction: the flip
  stratum is 54 pairs, and 27 both-match is its bar. No re-stratification
  after seeing the numbers.
